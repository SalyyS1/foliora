# Security policy

Foliora is in development. Do not put personal documents, vault keys, access
tokens, or other sensitive material in issues, pull requests, fixtures, or
logs.

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the repository maintainers. Until
a contact address is published, open a minimal issue asking for a private
reporting channel; do not disclose exploit details publicly.

The project does not claim to protect against a compromised operating system,
keylogger, or physical destruction of storage. Security claims require evidence
from the relevant release gate.
